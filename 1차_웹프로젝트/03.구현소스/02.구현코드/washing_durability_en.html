Anti-shrink Agent(MS-450/MX-27N)

ABOUT PRODUCT

MS-450 is a glyoxal resin, and MX-27N is a catalyst for thermosetting resin used together with this resin.

The two products form a set and are used for resin finishing of woven and knit cellulosic fibers and blended fabrics.

KEY FEATURES
MS-450
  • Provides excellent functionality including anti-wrinkle, anti-shrink, and heat resistance
  • Low free formaldehyde content, meeting the OEKO-TEX standard (<75ppm)
  • Effect and durability maintained even after washing, compatible with most processing additives
  • Excellent wrinkle recovery and shrinkage control properties
MX-27N
  • Provides excellent wrinkle and shrinkage resistance when used as a resin catalyst
  • Highly effective with low/non-formaldehyde thermosetting resins
  • Suppresses reduction in tear strength even when used with formaldehyde-based resins
  • Prevents whiteness loss after processing
TRUST & QUALITY

MX-27N is a bluesign-certified product, providing a textile finishing solution that considers both people and the environment.
