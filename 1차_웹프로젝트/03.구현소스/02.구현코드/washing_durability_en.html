Anti-shrink Agent(MS-450/MX-27N)

ABOUT PRODUCT

ERUSAM RESIN MS-450 is a glyoxal resin, and ERUSAM RESIN FIXER MX-27N is a catalyst for thermosetting resin used together with this resin.

The two products form a set and are used for resin finishing of woven and knit cellulosic fibers and blended fabrics.

KEY FEATURES

MS-450: Provides excellent functionality including anti-wrinkle, anti-shrink, and heat resistance

MS-450: Low free formaldehyde content, meeting the OEKO-TEX standard (<75ppm)

MS-450: Effect and durability maintained even after washing, compatible with most processing additives

MS-450: Excellent wrinkle recovery and shrinkage control properties

MX-27N: Provides excellent wrinkle and shrinkage resistance when used as a resin catalyst

MX-27N: Highly effective with low/non-formaldehyde thermosetting resins

MX-27N: Suppresses reduction in tear strength even when used with formaldehyde-based resins

MX-27N: Prevents whiteness loss after processing

TRUST & QUALITY

ERUSAM RESIN MS-450 and RESIN FIXER MX-27N are bluesign-certified products, providing a textile finishing solution that considers both people and the environment.
