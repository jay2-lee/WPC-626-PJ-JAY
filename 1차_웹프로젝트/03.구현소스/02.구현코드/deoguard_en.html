Deodorizing/Antibacterial(DEOGUARD)

PRODUCT FEATURES

Chemical Composition : Organic + Inorganic hybrid compound

Appearance : Blue liquid

Acidity : Weakly acidic (pH : 4.5 ± 1.0)

Ionicity : Nonionic (Nonion)

Excellent deodorizing effect against a wide range of everyday odors

Fast-acting formula enables deodorization within a short period of time

A deodorizing / antibacterial agent that provides excellent washing durability

Demonstrates excellent antiviral efficacy against Feline calicivirus (FCV) and Influenza A virus H3N2 (IAV-H3N2)

Superior antibacterial performance against a broad spectrum of bacteria

High thermal stability and heat durability, applicable to a wide variety of fabrics
