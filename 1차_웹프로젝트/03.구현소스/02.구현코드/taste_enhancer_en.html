Curcumin

ABOUT PRODUCT

ERUSAM Curcumin PLUS is a product made by separately extracting curcumin, the core active compound found in turmeric, and turning it into a powder.

Unlike ordinary turmeric powder, which is simply ground from the raw root, this product is different in that it is concentrated with curcumin alone and processed for better absorption.

Curcumin is naturally fat-soluble and does not dissolve well in water, but this product undergoes a multi-step solubilization process to become a water-soluble form that allows for fast absorption in the body.

With almost no taste or smell, it can be mixed into water or food without any discomfort, making it a health routine product you can adjust to your own lifestyle.

TRUST & QUALITY

The raw material is turmeric extract from India, and each serving contains 2,000mg of curcumin.

It is manufactured at a specialized facility certified with Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) for health functional foods, ensuring reliable quality control.

Processed to be tasteless and odorless, it can be consumed comfortably by both children and adults, which is another key factor of trust.

HOW TO USE

The most basic method is to mix about 3g once a day with warm water and drink it.

You can also apply it in everyday life by adding 1 teaspoon when cooking rice to make turmeric rice, adding it as a topping for yogurt, salad, or baking, or mixing it into smoothies or juice as an energizing drink.

A dedicated measuring spoon is provided to help you measure the exact intake amount.
