Formaldehyde Catcher

ABOUT PRODUCT

With the increasing density of modern housing and the rise of newly constructed buildings, Formaldehyde released from indoor spaces causes headaches, dizziness and other symptoms, known as 'Sick House Syndrome', which has become a social issue.

Nevertheless, various materials containing formaldehyde are still being indiscriminately used in building materials and daily household products.

FORMALDEHYDE CATCHER is an eco-friendly functional product developed to address these social issues and comply with F☆☆☆☆ (JAS Standards).

It effectively suppresses or removes formaldehyde by being applied or mixed into building material surfaces.

Furthermore, this product is utilized not only in the construction materials sector but also in various industrial fields such as textiles, paints, adhesives, resins, and interior materials.

FORMALDEHYDE CATCHER SERIES is a high-performance additive that chemically bonds and neutralizes residual formaldehyde.

Effective at suppressing formaldehyde even with small amounts, it has excellent compatibility with a wide range of resins.
