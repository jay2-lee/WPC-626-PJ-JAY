DURASIL

Hybrid Resin utilizing the special properties of
Silicone graft polymerization

DURASIL is a graft polymerization resin based on silicone.
Our polymerization technology enables the polymerization of silicone and acrylic functional resins.

Abrasion resistance, weather resistance, water repellency, oil repellency, noise sensitivity polymerization,
and other properties of M/S, Compounds, Liquid, Powders are being requested for design and technical trials
as external auxiliary materials.
