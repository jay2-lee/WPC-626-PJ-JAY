Wear & Slip Resistance Agent(DURASIL)

ABOUT PRODUCT

DURASIL is a graft polymerization hybrid resin based on silicone.

By applying technology that polymerizes silicone with other resins such as acrylic, it is provided as a customized material for plastics, films, sheets, and synthetic leather requiring abrasion resistance, scratch resistance, and sliding properties.

PRODUCT LINE

It is composed of three types depending on application and form.

R type: Powder form, mixed with oil-based paints and plastics

D type: Soluble in oil-based solvents (MEK, DMF, etc.), used for films, sheets, and oil-based coatings

E type: Aqueous emulsion form, used for water-based paints and textile finishing agents

PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS

Proven effectiveness through actual testing.

Synthetic leather with R-170S added showed no damage even after 12,000 abrasion cycles

With 10% addition of LC-190 (E type), scratch resistance improved from about 10 cycles to about 5,000 cycles

With EEA resin application, an increase in oxygen index confirmed improved flame retardancy
