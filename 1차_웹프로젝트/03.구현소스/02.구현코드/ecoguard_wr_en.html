Non-fluorinated Water Repellent(ECOGUARD-WR)

ABOUT PRODUCT

ECOGUARD-WR is an eco-friendly non-fluorinated water repellent agent that does not contain harmful substances such as PFAS or APEO.

It can be used at a low temperature of around 150℃ and provides superior water repellency and washing durability compared to conventional wax and silicone-based repellents.

KEY FEATURES

Free of harmful substances such as PFAS and APEO

Usable at low temperature (150℃); superior water repellency and washing durability compared to wax/silicone repellents

Provides a soft touch and antistatic property; can also be used as a flexible water repellent finish for napped fabrics

Achieves durable water repellency on Polyester, Cotton, Rayon, Nylon, and their blends

Weakly cationic product; not recommended for combined use with anionic agents

Usable across a wide pH range, though a weakly acidic condition is recommended for higher adsorption rate

Used by diluting with water as in general finishing methods, followed by a Drying–Curing process

SPECIFICATIONS

The appearance is a milky white emulsion, with pH of 2.0–6.0 and ionicity of Weak Cationic. Specific gravity is 0.99±0.01, solid content is 25±1.0%, and it dilutes easily in cold water.

HOW TO USE

Recommended usage amounts and processing conditions by fabric type are as follows.

Fabric Padding Tentering
Cotton 30~80 g/L 170℃ x 180sec
T/C, T/R 30~60 g/L 180℃ x 180sec
C/N 30~60 g/L 170℃ x 120sec
Nylon 30~60 g/L 170℃ x 120sec
N/PC, N/C 30~60 g/L 170℃ x 120sec

For optimal performance, fabrics should be thoroughly pre-treated to remove residues such as penetrants, dyeing auxiliaries, softeners, and surfactants. Fabric pH should be kept within 5–6 and should not exceed 7 to avoid reduced water repellency or gum-up issues.

If performance is insufficient after one treatment, re-process 2–3 times.

ADDITIONAL NOTES

If excellent washing durability is required, it is recommended to use it together with ERUSAM Cross-Linker. The recommended ratio is ECOGUARD-WR 100pt + ECOGUARD-CL 10–15pt (10–15% relative to the repellent).

Applying water repellent finishing to fabrics dyed with disperse dyes may reduce sublimation fastness, so it is advisable to select dyes with excellent sublimation fastness.

PACKAGING & STORAGE

Packaged in IBC Tank units, and should be stored sealed at 5–30℃, away from direct sunlight.
